## Human idiom

A thumbnail is a poster that must work at 160 px wide.
Face + three words beats a pretty landscape.
High contrast. Cut the midtones. Crop tighter than you think.
Title on the still and title in the metadata are different jobs. Do not duplicate the whole sentence on the picture.

## Agent translation

Canvas 1280×720 (YouTube) unless they name another.
Hero is a pasted face or object. Offset to put the eyes on the upper third.
Type: 2–4 words, size 72–96, one color + a dark stroke-substitute (a rect behind the words).
The harness cannot stroke text. Fake a stroke with a darker `draw rect` slab, or a second text draw offset by 2 px (ugly; prefer the slab).
